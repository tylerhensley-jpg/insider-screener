Insider Moves

Source: SEC Form 4 filings (open-market purchases, code P, and sales, code S). Planned 10b5-1 sales appear only when they cut a large share of the insider's holdings. Grants, option exercises, tax withholding and gifts are excluded.

Timing record: for each earlier buy or discretionary sale (trades within 30 days merged into one), the stock's return over the next 6 months versus the S&P 500 (SPY). A buy counts as well timed if the stock beat SPY; a sale, if it trailed. Range position is where the price sat between its 52-week low (0%) and high (100%). Holdings counts exclude unvested options.
